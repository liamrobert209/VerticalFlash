Ocushield — Definitive Competitor List
Competitors by product line, split by UK and Europe markets
Prepared for Liam, Ocushield  ·  September 2026
How to read this document
This is a working competitor map across the six product lines Ocushield competes in: anti-blue light glasses/eyewear, anti-blue light screen protectors, privacy filters for monitors, supplements, anti-blue light bulbs, and red light therapy. Each product line has a UK ranking, a wider-Europe ranking, and a US ranking, up to the top 10 brands by overall brand presence in that category. Brands that recur across lists (e.g. Bon Charge, BlockBlueLight, CurrentBody) are your broadest, most direct competitors.
Follower counts marked with * are approximate estimates. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok block automated reads, and follower aggregators were unreliable at the time of research, so figures should be treated as order-of-magnitude and directional rather than exact — verify live before using any single number in a report. Brand names, websites and social handles are verified. Where a handle could not be confirmed it is marked “not found”. A ⚑ marks brands that compete with Ocushield across multiple product lines.
Note: this document is deliberately the clean competitor list only. The ad-intelligence layer (value propositions, ad angles, and where/how each brand runs ads) is summarised briefly at the end as suggested next steps.

Cross-category direct competitors
A handful of brands compete with Ocushield on more than one product line and should be treated as primary head-to-head competitors:
• BlockBlueLight (blockbluelight.co.uk) — UK-based; glasses + bulbs + red light + screen wellness. The single most overlapping direct competitor.
• Bon Charge (boncharge.com) — glasses + bulbs + red light; premium biohacking positioning.
• CurrentBody (currentbody.com) — dominant UK/EU beauty-tech retailer and the brand to watch in red light therapy.
• PanzerGlass (panzerglass.com) — the biggest EU-native player straddling both screen protectors and privacy filters.
• Vitabiotics (Visionace) & MacuShield / Nutrof (Théa) — the incumbent scale players on the supplements side (UK and Europe respectively).

Since you’re using this to brief an ad-building system, the fastest way to pull each competitor’s live value propositions and creative angles is:

• Google Ads Transparency Center — search each domain to see their search and YouTube ads.
