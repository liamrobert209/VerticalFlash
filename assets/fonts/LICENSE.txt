Fraunces-Black.ttf, Manrope-Regular.ttf, Manrope-Bold.ttf

These are Google Fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version
1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which explicitly permits bundling,
redistribution, and embedding in software. Fetched directly from Google's
font CDN (fonts.gstatic.com).

Used as free stand-ins for the brand guideline's actual typefaces (Quincy
CF and Filson Pro), both confirmed no longer available for purchase —
Quincy CF's MyFonts listing literally states "The font is no longer
available for purchase"; Filson Pro was reported the same on Adobe Fonts.
Fraunces was chosen specifically as the closest free match to Quincy CF's
soft, rounded-terminal display-serif character (replacing an earlier,
stiffer Bitter substitute). See src/lib/static-ad-fonts.ts.
